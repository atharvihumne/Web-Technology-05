 Aim

To study the basic concepts of the Internet, E-mail, WWW, Broadband and FTP.

 Problem Statement

To understand the basic working and uses of the Internet and its important services such as E-mail, WWW, Broadband and FTP.

Theory

The Internet is a worldwide network that connects computers and other devices. It helps us communicate, search for information, share files and access online services.

E-mail (Electronic Mail) is used to send and receive messages through the Internet. It is commonly used for personal, educational and professional communication.

WWW (World Wide Web) is a collection of websites and web pages that can be accessed through the Internet using a web browser. It was invented by **Tim Berners-Lee**.

Broadband** provides high-speed Internet access. It can be provided through fiber optic, wireless, DSL, cable and satellite connections.

FTP (File Transfer Protocol)** is used to transfer files between computers over a network. It allows users to upload files to a server and download files from a server.

 Conclusion

Thus, the basic concepts of the Internet and its services such as E-mail, WWW, Broadband and FTP were studied successfully.
